---
title: Domaine des 2 Ânes, Premiers Pas
---
Eric Asimov, the nephew of Isaac Asimov and more importantly one of the wine critics at the New York Times, published a [great list](https://www.nytimes.com/2025/01/25/dining/drinks/best-cheap-wine-under-20.html) of wines under $20 he recommends last month.

I bought a few of them, and ended up really liking the "Premier Pas" Carignan from Domaine des 2 Ânes. It's not going to win any awards, but it's a good bottle for your next date night or dinner party.
